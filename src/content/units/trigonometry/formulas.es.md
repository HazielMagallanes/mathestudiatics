| Razón    | Fórmula                                                                                |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Seno     | $\sin\theta = \frac{\text{opuesto}}{\text{hipotenusa}}$                                |
| Coseno   | $\cos\theta = \frac{\text{adyacente}}{\text{hipotenusa}}$                              |
| Tangente | $\tan\theta = \frac{\text{opuesto}}{\text{adyacente}} = \frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}$ |

## Identidad fundamental y derivadas

$$\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1$$

$$ 1 + \tan^2\theta = \sec^2\theta, \qquad
\cot\theta = \frac{1}{\tan\theta} = \frac{\cos\theta}{\sin\theta}$$

## Valores exactos frecuentes

| $\theta$ | $0$ | $30°$ | $45°$ | $60°$ | $90°$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $\theta$ en radianes | $0$ | $\frac{\pi}{6}$ | $\frac{\pi}{4}$ | $\frac{\pi}{3}$ | $\frac{\pi}{2}$ |
| $\sin\theta$ | $0$ | $\frac{1}{2}$ | $\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ | $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ | $1$ |
| $\cos\theta$ | $1$ | $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ | $\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ | $\frac{1}{2}$ | $0$ |
| $\tan\theta$ | $0$ | $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ | $1$ | $\sqrt{3}$ | no definida |

## Conversión de ángulos

$$180° = \pi \text{ radianes}, \qquad
\theta_{\text{rad}} = \theta_{\text{deg}} \cdot \frac{\pi}{180}$$

## Gráficas

$$y = a\sin(bx) + d \Rightarrow \text{amplitud} = \left|a\right|, \qquad
\text{período} = \frac{2\pi}{\left|b\right|}$$
