| Razón    | Fórmula                                                                                |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Seno     | $\sin\theta = \frac{\text{opuesto}}{\text{hipotenusa}}$                                |
| Coseno   | $\cos\theta = \frac{\text{adyacente}}{\text{hipotenusa}}$                              |
| Tangente | $\tan\theta = \frac{\text{opuesto}}{\text{adyacente}} = \frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}$ |

## Identidad fundamental y derivadas

$$\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1$$

$$
\begin{gathered}
1 + \tan^2\theta = \sec^2\theta \\
\cot\theta = \frac{1}{\tan\theta} = \frac{\cos\theta}{\sin\theta}
\end{gathered}
$$

## Conversión de ángulos

$$
\begin{gathered}
180° = \pi \text{ radianes} \\
\theta_{\text{rad}} = \theta_{\text{deg}} \cdot \frac{\pi}{180}
\end{gathered}
$$
